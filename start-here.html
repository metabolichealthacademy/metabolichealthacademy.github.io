Insulin resistance and prediabetes

Insulin helps glucose move from the bloodstream into cells for energy. Insulin resistance means the body does not respond to insulin as effectively as it should. Prediabetes means blood glucose is higher than normal but not yet in the diabetes range.

Start here to understand how these concepts are related, why they often cause no obvious symptoms, and when screening may be discussed with a healthcare professional.

How to use this pathway safely

  • Use these materials to build general understanding and prepare questions.
  • Discuss screening, symptoms, test results, diagnoses, and treatment decisions with a qualified healthcare professional who knows your history.
  • Seek urgent or emergency care for severe or rapidly worsening symptoms rather than relying on educational content.
