Privacy Policy

A plain-language description of current website data practices.

Current site operation

This is a public informational website. It does not currently provide user accounts, patient portals, medical-record uploads, or personalized clinical services.

Information you provide

If you contact us by email, your email provider and ours process the information you send. We may retain correspondence as reasonably needed to respond, document corrections, or protect the website.

Do not submit health information

Do not send protected health information, medical records, account credentials, or urgent medical questions. Email is not a clinical communication channel.

Hosting and technical data

The site is hosted through GitHub Pages and may use Cloudflare services. These providers may process routine technical data such as IP address, browser information, request timing, and security events under their own policies.

Cookies and analytics

The published site does not intentionally set advertising cookies or operate a proprietary user-tracking system. If analytics or newsletter services are added, this policy should be updated before activation and any consent requirements addressed.

Contact

Privacy questions may be sent to info@themetabolichealthacademy.com.
