About

Our mission is to help people make sense of metabolic health information and prepare for better conversations with their healthcare professionals.

Our purpose

The Metabolic Health Academy translates medical research, clinical guidance, and foundational health concepts into understandable public education. Topics include insulin resistance, prediabetes, type 2 diabetes, obesity, hypertension, lipid disorders, metabolic liver disease, sleep apnea, cardiovascular risk, nutrition, movement, and healthy aging.

Our educational model

Content is intended for general education. It does not provide individualized diagnosis, treatment, medication changes, or emergency guidance. Readers should discuss personal medical decisions with their own qualified healthcare professionals.
